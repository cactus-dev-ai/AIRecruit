# AIRecruit
AI 招聘，通过岗位画像和 RAG 知识库，对简历进行评分，初选出大约 30% 候选人，发送面试通知，候选人拿到通知后，在线进入语音面试，AI 机器人通过岗位和简历得内容，进行 30 分钟得交流，最终得到候选人详细得报告，发送给部门负责人做最终得确认。
AI recruitment leverages job profiling and a RAG knowledge base to score resumes. It shortlists approximately 30% of candidates and sends out interview invitations. Upon receiving the notifications, candidates join an online voice interview. Guided by the job profile and resume information, the AI robot conducts a 30-minute conversation and generates a detailed candidate report, which is then forwarded to department heads for final validation.
## 术语注解

1. job profiling：岗位画像（HR/AI 招聘领域标准说法）
2. RAG knowledge base：RAG 知识库（Retrieval-Augmented Generation，保留缩写，技术文档通用）
3. score resumes：对简历评分
4. shortlist：动词，筛选出、列入候选名单（招聘专业词，优于单纯 select）
5. interview invitations：面试通知
6. voice interview：语音面试
7. department heads：部门负责人
8. final validation：最终确认
